Not one fix. The parser fix leaves the other two broken: the booking-v3 hint keeps asking for the month first, and the one-day moves have no month in them to flip.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so “7 April” comes out as 4 July. Every channel, every tenant. This is what #4471 already says.

hint: booking-v3’s date field asks for the wrong order. Under its date field the v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what it shows, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports sent through the form of a tenant on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it; the hint does.

one-day: a date one day off. No day and month swap in these, a shift of one day: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” (R-41202), “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” (R-41344), “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” (R-41236). That reads as a time zone or day-boundary bug, not a date-order one. The desk had already listed these in desk-notes.json; the cause table filed them under parser because of their tenant.

How the count is made

  • One id, one report: the export repeats rows across its pages, and the repeats are dropped.
  • parser and hint come from causes.ts (tenant locale, booking form and channel), never from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant both ways.
  • one-day is the desk’s own list in desk-notes.json; each of those bodies was read.
  • The subject line does not tell them apart: “Month and day swapped?” heads reports of all three problems, so merging on subject (decision 0001) put them in one ticket.
